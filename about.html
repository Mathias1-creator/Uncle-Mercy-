Paul's Story

Paul started playing guitar in his 20s, chasing that big 80s sound like everybody else with a poster of Slash on the wall. Then life did what life does — work, family, years flying by — and the guitar spent way too long in its case.

One day he picked it back up. Then he made some calls. Five guys, a garage, and a shared belief that the world still needs "Paradise City" played at full volume — and UNCLE MERCY was born. Six years later, we're still rocking stages all over the North Bay, and we're louder than ever.

Turns out you can walk away from rock and roll for a while. But it never walks away from you.

Proudly Local. Sonoma Born.

We're not a band that flies in for the gig. We live here. UNCLE MERCY was born and raised in Sonoma County, and we play for the same community we're part of.

That's why we treat every show like it matters — because around here, the crowd isn't strangers. It's neighbors, friends, and family. And nobody phones it in for family.

From Clearlake down to Fremont — Santa Rosa, Petaluma, Napa, Marin, the East Bay — if you're in the North Bay, you're on our home turf.
